# PULSE — Instant Vitals Scanner (PPT & Presentation Guide)

> **Slide Title:** Instant Vitals Scanner — Zero-Hardware Health Monitoring  
> **Subtitle:** Turning any smartphone camera & flashlight into a clinical-grade pulse oximeter.

---

## 1. Slide Layout (3-Column Presentation Structure)

### Column 1: The Problem & The Breakthrough
* **The Problem:** 
  Wearables like Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, or Fitbit cost ₹15,000 to ₹40,000. Over 80% of elderly patients in India do not own or wear smartwatches.
* **Our Breakthrough:** 
  **Zero hardware required.** Patients simply place their index fingertip over their phone’s camera and flashlight (or laptop webcam) for 14 seconds to capture their full vital signs.

---

### Column 2: How It Works (Simple Science in Plain English)
1. **Light Penetration:** 
   The phone's flashlight (or laptop screen light) illuminates the capillary bed just under the skin of the fingertip.
2. **Arterial Pulse Wave:** 
   With every heartbeat, a fresh wave of oxygenated blood surges through the capillaries, temporarily absorbing more light.
3. **Camera Sensor (PPG):** 
   The camera samples microscopic color variations at 30 frames per second using **Photoplethysmography (PPG)**.
4. **Digital Filtering & Autocorrelation:** 
   An intelligent signal processing engine filters out hand tremors, room light flicker, and harmonic double-counting to extract pure cardiac rhythms.

---

### Column 3: Complete Vitals Suite (Measured in 14 Seconds)

| Vital Sign | Clinical Purpose | Optimal Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ❤️ **Heart Rate (BPM)** | Detects arrhythmia, tachycardia, and resting pulse spikes | 60 – 85 BPM |
| 🫁 **Blood Oxygen ($SpO_2$)** | Early indicator of respiratory illness and hypoxia | 95% – 99% |
| 🌬️ **Respiration Rate** | Monitored via cardiac Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia (RSA) | 12 – 20 BrPM |
| 💓 **HRV (RMSSD)** | Evaluates autonomic nervous system balance & recovery | 35 – 65 ms |
| 🧘 **Stress Level** | Evaluates mental & physiological fatigue index | Relaxed / Normal |

## 2. Key Hackathon Winning Points (Why Judges Care)

* 🚀 **Universal Accessibility:** Works on any standard ₹7,000 Android smartphone or budget laptop with a camera.
* 🛡️ **Instant Caregiver Alerts:** If vitals cross danger thresholds (e.g. resting BPM > 120 or $SpO_2$ < 92%), PULSE triggers an automated alert banner and audio notification for linked family members.
* 📈 **Persistent ECG Waveform History:** Every scan automatically saves the full graphical pulse wave and vitals log locally and to the cloud for doctor review.
